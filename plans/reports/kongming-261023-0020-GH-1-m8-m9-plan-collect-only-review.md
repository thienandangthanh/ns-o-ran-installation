## Outcome

**The plan is not Ready as written.** I found 1 blocker, 5 preflight-required items and 21 mitigations within the contract. None of my fixes is an outcome-change request. One exception: two of the three options for K1 would be outcome-change requests, and the recommended option is not.

The findings that would most likely break the unattended run or the stop clause:
1. **K1, blocker.** The M9 runner inherits `gate-m0-host.sh`. That script **creates a probe snapshot, then creates and deletes a branch snapshot** (`gate-m0-host.sh:40, 46, 49`), unattended and out of reach of the deny rule. This breaks the invariant "never delete a snapshot". It also falsifies P0's claim that "with `--no-snapshots` it never creates a snapshot".
2. **K2, preflight-required.** The M9 disk projection can be computed now, and it is very likely negative before P1 starts. Measured: host free is 75 G, and the fresh-install→f7 chain overlay is 39.7 GB allocated.
3. **K3 and K4, preflight-required.** Segment 1 dies at its first command. The opener's `virsh -c qemu:///system …` form matches no allow rule, and bare `virsh` resolves to `qemu:///session`, so the allowed forms would read the wrong hypervisor. Separately, P6 hits a certain T4 because neither 3GPP TS 36.323 nor 3GPP TS 38.323 is in Zotero.
4. **K5, R1(b) is infeasible as the plan writes it.** With 2 gNBs and the plan's precheck order, the second same-IMSI control is refused `alreadyOnTarget`, not `inHandover`, because `m_lastMmWaveCell` only changes when the handover completes.
5. **K6, K7, K8: R3 and M9 determinism and equality.** N6 certifies only `cellMetrics`, but QP also reads `UeMetrics`. The G1 mapping omits QP's `ue`/`servcell`/`nbcells` keys. M9 would compare a rebuilt QP image ID byte for byte, and that fails by construction.
